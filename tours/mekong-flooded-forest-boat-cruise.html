The experience

Glide through the extraordinary flooded forests of the Mekong River on a private longtail boat. This is one of Southeast Asia's most unique river ecosystems — a labyrinth of submerged trees, mirror-still waterways, and abundant birdlife that few travellers ever witness.

Your local guide will navigate through hidden channels between remote river islands, stopping to spot wildlife, witness a local fishing family along the river, and experience the stillness of one of Cambodia's last wild river frontiers.

How the tour runs

  1. Hotel pickup We collect you from your hotel or guesthouse in Stung Treng town by Lexus SUV.
  2. Board the longtail Life jackets on, and you set off on your private boat with your local guide.
  3. Into the flooded forest Wind through submerged trees and quiet channels between river islands, stopping for wildlife and birdlife.
  4. A fishing family on the river See how local families fish and live along the Mekong.
  5. Return and drop-off Back to the landing, then to your hotel in town.
