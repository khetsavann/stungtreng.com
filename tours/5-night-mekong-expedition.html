About this tour

Travel from Stung Treng to Phnom Penh over five nights along the Mekong. The first night is at the Koh Pdao Community Based Ecotourism site, followed by overnight stops in river towns, including Kampong Cham, before the expedition ends in Phnom Penh.

What happens on the tour

  1. Night 1Koh Pdao Community Based Ecotourism
  2. Night 2Krong Kracheh or Chhlong
  3. Night 3Krouch Chhmar or Stueng Trang District
  4. Night 4Krong Kampong Cham
  5. Night 5Phnom Penh

What's included

  • ✅ Private longtail boat with roof
  • ✅ Life jackets
  • ✅ Experience boat operator with over-twenty-year experience with the Mekong River

Questions about this tour

Where does the expedition end?

In Phnom Penh, on night five.

Why do two nights list two towns?

Night 2 is in Krong Kracheh or Chhlong, and night 3 in Krouch Chhmar or Stueng Trang District. We'll confirm your stops when you book.

Where does it start?

In Stung Treng. The first night is at the Koh Pdao Community Based Ecotourism site.
