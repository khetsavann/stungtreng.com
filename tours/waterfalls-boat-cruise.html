About this tour

The best of Stung Treng in a single day. Start with a morning boat cruise through the Mekong's flooded forests, then venture out to the province's thundering waterfalls — a hidden gem unknown to most Cambodia travellers.

Swim in the cold water of the beautiful island river beach, enjoy a fresh Khmer lunch prepared by a local family, and return to town by late afternoon. This is the tour most guests say was their favourite day in Cambodia.

What happens on the tour

  1. Morning pickupYour guide collects you from your hotel in Stung Treng town ($125 option).
  2. Boat cruise through the flooded forestGlide through the submerged trees and river channels of the Mekong.
  3. The waterfallsVisit the province's thundering waterfalls.
  4. Island river beachSwim in the cold water at a beautiful river island beach.
  5. Khmer lunch with a local familyEnjoy a fresh meal prepared by a local family.
  6. Afternoon cruise and returnA second boat cruise, then back to town by late afternoon.

What's included

  • ✅ Private longtail boat with roof & van transport
  • ✅ Drinking water
  • ✅ All entrance fees
  • ✅ Hotel pickup & drop-off by Lexus SUV in Stung Treng town ($125 option)
  • ✅ English-speaking local guide ($125 option only)
  • ✅ Fresh Khmer breakfast, coffee & lunch ($125 option only)

Questions about this tour

What's the difference between the $85 and $125 options?

At $85 you travel entirely by boat. At $125 you travel by Lexus SUV with two boat cruises, and the English-speaking guide, Khmer breakfast, coffee and lunch are included.

Can I swim?

Yes. The tour stops at a river island beach where you can swim.

When do we get back?

You return to Stung Treng town by late afternoon.

Is lunch included?

Breakfast, coffee and lunch are included in the $125 option only.
