About this tour

The definitive Stung Treng experience. Over four days, you'll explore every dimension of this extraordinary region — flooded forests by kayak and boat, thundering waterfalls, cycling through river islands, and deep-diving into authentic Khmer food culture with local families.

Accommodation is in a comfortable local guesthouse in Stung Treng town. Each day brings a different adventure — no two days repeat. This is not a packaged tour, it's a genuine immersion into one of Cambodia's most overlooked and beautiful provinces.

What happens on the tour

  1. Day 1 — ArrivalHotel pickup, check in to your guesthouse in Stung Treng town and a welcoming dinner.
  2. Day 2 — Flooded forestsKayak and cruise through the Mekong's flooded forests.
  3. Day 3 — WaterfallsHead out to the province's waterfalls.
  4. Day 4 — River islands and foodCycle through river islands and cook and eat with a local family, then transfer to your next stop.

What's included
