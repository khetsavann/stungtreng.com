About this tour

Spend four days on and beside the Mekong with a community homestay, kayaking and island camping. You start with a waterfall and a cruise through the flooded forest, then kayak between riverside villages and sleep out on the islands.

Along the way you meet villagers who live on the river and see how they live day to day, before returning to Stung Treng by boat.

What happens on the tour

  1. Day 1 — Waterfall, flooded forest and a homestayFrom O' Svay, visit a waterfall and have lunch. Return by boat to the flooded forest and a small island, then spend the night at a community homestay with dinner.
  2. Day 2 — Kayak to Vien Sien, camp at Koh HanKayak from O' Svay to Vien Sien village, see how the villagers live and look for dolphins, then continue to Koh Han for camping. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are included.
  3. Day 3 — Island life and Mekong kayakingVisit the people living on the island and walk around the village. In the afternoon, kayak along the Mekong in the flooded forest and camp on the island.
  4. Day 4 — Back to Stung TrengTravel back to Stung Treng by boat from Koh Han.

What's included

  • ✅ Boat and kayak trips as listed in the itinerary
  • ✅ 1 night at a community homestay (Day 1)
  • ✅ 2 nights camping on the islands (Days 2 and 3)
  • ✅ Day 1: lunch & dinner
  • ✅ Day 2: breakfast, lunch & dinner
  • ✅ Return boat to Stung Treng from Koh Han (Day 4)

Questions about this tour

Where do we sleep?

The first night is at a community homestay. Nights two and three are camping on the islands.

How do we get back to Stung Treng?

On Day 4 you travel back to Stung Treng by boat from Koh Han.

Will I see dolphins?

The route passes Vien Sien village, where dolphins can be seen. As with all wildlife, sightings can't be guaranteed.

How is this different from the Ultimate Nature & Food Tour?

This trip is more remote, with a homestay and camping on the islands. The Ultimate Nature & Food Tour is based in a guesthouse in Stung Treng town.
