About this tour

Glide through the extraordinary flooded forests of the Mekong River on a private longtail boat. This is one of Southeast Asia's most unique river ecosystems — a labyrinth of submerged trees, mirror-still waterways, and abundant birdlife that few travellers ever witness.

Your local guide navigates hidden channels between remote river islands, stopping to spot wildlife, meet a local fishing family along the river, and take in the stillness of the river.

What happens on the tour

  1. StartMeet your guide and board the private longtail boat (roofed, with life jackets for everyone).
  2. Into the flooded forestLeave the main river and enter the narrow channels between submerged trees and river islands.
  3. Wildlife and birdlifeYour guide stops the boat to spot birds and other wildlife and explains how the forest floods each year.
  4. A local fishing familySee how a family on the river fishes and lives with the seasons.
  5. ReturnCruise back and finish after about 4 hours.

Questions about this tour

Which departure time should I choose?

Both morning and afternoon departures are available. Tell us your preferred time when you book and we'll confirm it.

Is it suitable for beginners and families?

Yes. You sit in a roofed boat with life jackets provided, and no physical effort is needed.

What's the difference between the $45 and $60 options?

The tour itself is identical. The $60 option adds hotel pickup and drop-off by Lexus SUV within Stung Treng town.

Do you also offer kayaking?

Yes — see our Kayaking & Boat Cruise, which combines paddling with the boat.
