About this tour

Combine the thrill of kayaking with a relaxing boat cruise through the Mekong's flooded forest. Paddle your own kayak through narrow jungle waterways inaccessible by motor boat — then switch to the longtail for wider stretches and island hopping.

This tour is perfect for active travellers who want both adventure and serenity. No prior kayaking experience needed — the channels are calm and beginner-friendly.

What happens on the tour

  1. Getting thereTravel to the kayaking point — by scooter on your own ($65 option) or by Lexus SUV ($75 option).
  2. Kayak the narrow channelsPaddle your own kayak through jungle waterways that motor boats can't enter. Kayaks, paddles and life jackets are provided.
  3. Switch to the longtail boatBoard the private roofed longtail boat for the wider stretches of the flooded forest.
  4. Island hoppingYour guide takes you between remote river islands, stopping for wildlife and views.
  5. ReturnFinish after about 4 hours.

What's included

  • ✅ Kayaks, paddles & life jackets provided
  • ✅ Private longtail boat with roof
  • ✅ English-speaking local guide
  • ✅ Drinking water
  • ✅ All entrance fees
  • ✅ Lexus SUV ride & hotel pickup/drop-off in Stung Treng town ($75 option only)

Questions about this tour

Do I need kayaking experience?

No. The channels are calm and beginner-friendly, and your guide helps you get started.

What's the difference between the $65 and $75 options?

The tour itself is the same. At $65 you travel to the kayaking point by scooter on your own; at $75 the Lexus SUV ride is included.

Is it a morning tour?

Yes, it departs in the morning. We'll confirm the exact time when you book.

See our Boat Cruise, which stays on the longtail boat the whole time.
